Frequently Asked Questions

How many sections in SSC CGL Tier-I?

Tier-I has 4 sections: General Intelligence, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude and English Comprehension.

Is there negative marking in SSC CGL?

Yes, 0.50 marks are deducted for each wrong answer in both Tier-I and Tier-II.
