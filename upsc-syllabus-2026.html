Frequently Asked Questions

How many papers in UPSC Mains?

There are 9 papers in Mains. Two are qualifying (English and Indian Language), the remaining 7 count toward merit.

What is the UPSC total marks?

Mains is 1750 marks and the interview is 275 marks, making a total of 2025 marks.
