Documents to Carry

Candidates must bring original documents along with self-attested photocopies for verification.

  • arrow_right Matriculation / equivalent certificates
  • arrow_right Graduation / post-graduation marksheets
  • arrow_right Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS)
  • arrow_right Photo ID proof (Aadhaar/PAN/Voter ID)
  • arrow_right Passport-size photographs
  • arrow_right Discharge certificate (for ex-servicemen, if applicable)

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are SSC document verification notices published?

On the official SSC website (ssc.gov.in) under the respective region (North/West/East/KMPR etc.).

What if I miss my document verification date?

Contact your SSC regional office immediately. Missed verification usually leads to cancellation of candidature.

Are photocopies required?

Yes, carry original documents with self-attested photocopies as mentioned in the notice.
