Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I apply for UP Scholarship 2026-27?

Apply online at the official National Scholarship Portal: scholarships.gov.in. There is no offline application.

Is the application fee charged?

No. The scholarship application is free of cost. Beware of agents asking for money.

What documents are required?

Aadhaar, bank account details, income certificate, previous marksheet, category certificate (if applicable) and photo are typically required.

When does the application window open?

The window opens after the state notification each year, typically around July-August. Check the portal for the exact schedule.
