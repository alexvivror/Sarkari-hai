Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I download my CTET certificate?

From the official CBSE CTET portal: ctet.nic.in. Look for the e-Certificate download section.

How long is the CTET certificate valid?

The CTET certificate is valid for 7 years from the date of declaration of result.

Is there a fee to download?

No, downloading the e-certificate is free.
